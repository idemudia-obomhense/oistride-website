1. INTRODUCTION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
This Privacy Policy explains how OIStride Ltd ("OIStride," "we," "us," or
"our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you
visit our website or use our Services.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  - Account information: full name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number,
    password (stored securely, never in plain text)
  - Enrollment information: which program(s) you've enrolled in, cohort
    selection, payment plan chosen, payment status
  - Payment information: handled directly by Paystack, our payment
    processor — OIStride does not receive or store your full card or bank
    account details. We receive confirmation of payment and a transaction
    reference from Paystack.
  - Communications: messages you send us via contact forms, email, or
    WhatsApp, and any information you provide when booking a
    consultation via Calendly
  - Usage data: basic information about how you use our website (such as
    pages visited), collected automatically

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
We use your information to:
  - create and manage your account
  - process your enrollment and payment
  - send you emails related to your enrollment (confirmations, payment
    reminders, program updates)
  - add you to the relevant cohort community (e.g. WhatsApp group) once
    you've enrolled
  - respond to questions you send us
  - improve our website and Services
  - meet our legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell your personal data.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
4. WHO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION WITH
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
We share information with the following third-party service providers,
solely to the extent needed for them to provide their service to us:
  - Paystack — payment processing
  - Supabase — account authentication and enrollment records
  - Resend — sending transactional emails (confirmations, reminders)
  - Calendly — consultation scheduling
  - Vercel — website hosting

Each of these providers has its own privacy practices governing how they
handle data on our behalf. We do not share your information with any
other third party for their own marketing purposes.

6. YOUR RIGHTS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), and where applicable
other privacy laws, you have the right to:
  - request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  - request correction of inaccurate data
  - request deletion of your data, subject to our legal and operational
    obligations (for example, we may need to retain payment records)
  - withdraw consent for optional communications, such as our newsletter,
    at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at oistride12@gmail.com.
