About OIStride

Learn by doing — that's the whole idea.

OIStride exists because career growth, practical PM skill, and AI fluency are usually sold by three different vendors — when for one person, they're actually the same journey.

Mission

Package proven practice, not unproven ideas

Every framework taught at OIStride has been used on real projects — not adapted from a textbook. The company monetizes already-delivered expertise: a curriculum that's been taught before, coaching frameworks that have worked before, and an AI-built product that's already live.

Vision

A trusted home for practical growth

To become a trusted platform for practical learning, career growth, product thinking, and AI-powered building — for professionals who want to do the work, not just watch a video about it.
