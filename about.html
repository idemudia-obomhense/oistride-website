About OIStride Academy

Learn by doing. That's the whole idea.

OIStride Academy is a practice-led technology-skills academy built to help people develop practical capabilities they can use in the real world. We bring structured learning, real-world cases, guided practice and practitioner feedback together so learning becomes something you can apply, not just something you know.

Current pathways include Agile Project Management, Product Management and AI Product Management, with more technology-skills programs to grow with the Academy.

Mission

Build practical capability, not just theoretical knowledge.

To equip people with practical tech skills they can confidently apply in the real world through practitioner-led, hands-on learning.

OIStride Academy exists to make technology-skills learning more practical, relevant and applicable. We connect concepts to real problems, frameworks to decisions, and learning to action.

Every framework taught at OIStride Academy has already been used on real projects, not adapted from a textbook. A curriculum that's been taught before. Coaching frameworks that have worked before. An AI-assisted build workflow that's already shipped something real. Nothing here is theory waiting to be tested on you.

Vision

A trusted home for practical growth

To become a trusted home for practical technology learning, helping people across Africa and beyond build the skills and confidence to do meaningful work in a technology-driven world.

How We Learn

Learning should change the way you work, not just what you know.

  • 1 Learn. Build strong foundational knowledge.
  • 2 Practice. Apply it through real-world exercises and live case studies.
  • 3 Get Feedback. Get direct, practitioner feedback on how you think and apply what you've learned.
  • 4 Apply. Put the skill to work in a real scenario, project or decision.
  • 5 Demonstrate. Show the capability you've built, with a certificate that reflects work you actually did.

This is the learning philosophy behind OIStride Academy: real learning happens when knowledge becomes action.
