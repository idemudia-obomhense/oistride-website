About OIStride

Learn by doing. That's the whole idea.

Career growth, practical project management skill, and AI fluency are usually sold by three different vendors. For one person, they're actually the same journey. OIStride exists to be the one place that treats them that way.

An AI-assisted build workflow that's already shipped something real. Nothing here is theory waiting to be tested on you.

Meet Jed

Obomhense Idemudia

Jed is a Senior Product Manager and Agile trainer who has spent his career at the intersection of product development, project delivery, and digital transformation, across fintech, healthcare, and travel-tech. He has trained and mentored professionals across Nigeria, Canada, the UK, the USA, and West Africa.

His approach comes from one belief: learning should change the way you work, not just what you know. Every session he runs is built around real business scenarios and industry-standard tools, not slides.
