### What You'll Walk Away With
**Heading:** Real capability, not just notes
- Run a live sprint in Jira: backlog, estimation, sprint boards, and burndown reporting.
- Build and defend a project schedule under real constraints: scope cuts, budget pressure, shifting stakeholders.
- Lead stakeholder and risk conversations through case studies drawn from real cross-industry scenarios.

### Curriculum (module descriptions, tightened, no fabricated detail added beyond what's real)

**Module 1: Foundations of Project Management**
What a PM actually does day to day: project life cycle, roles, and key terminology across the discipline.
Case study: Setting up a project right.

**Module 2: Planning, Communication, Cost & Risk**
Scope, estimation, dependencies, and the critical path. Building a realistic schedule and budget, and managing stakeholder communication as constraints change.
Case study: Planning under real constraints.

**Module 3: Execution, Monitoring & Closure**
Running kick-offs and managing a team through delivery. Tracking schedule, budget, and quality, then reporting progress and formally closing a project.
Case study: Execution to close-out.

**Module 4: Foundations of Agile & Scrum**
The Agile Manifesto, its principles and values, and how Agile compares to Waterfall and hybrid approaches in practice.
Case study: Moving a team from Waterfall to Agile.

**Module 5: Advanced Scrum with Jira**
Setting up projects and issue types in Jira, writing user stories, estimating with story points, and running sprint reports.
Case study: Running a live sprint in Jira.

**Module 6: Agile Project Management with Trello**
Sprint planning and backlog refinement, burndown and burnup tracking, and Kanban boards for visual workflow management.
Case study: Visual workflow management.

**Module 7: Agile Metrics & Reporting**
Velocity, burnup, and burndown as performance signals, and using metrics to drive continuous improvement and report effectively to stakeholders.
Case study: Continuous improvement in action.

**Module 8: Scrum Master Skills & Wrap-Up**
Servant leadership and team facilitation, coaching Agile teams, removing blockers, and conflict resolution.
Case study: Servant leadership in action.
