Who OIStride is for

This is for you if:

  • You want structured, case-study-driven learning, not another self-paced video library you'll never finish.
  • You're switching careers, formalising real PM experience, or ready to ship something with AI.
  • You want direct access to a practitioner, not just a curriculum.

This may not be the right time if:

  • You're looking for a passive credential with no live component.
  • You can't commit a few hours a week for the cohort duration.
  • You need something free — OIStride is a paid, high-touch program.
