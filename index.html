What We Do

Practical technology skills for the work ahead.

Technology keeps changing. The skills people need to work, build and lead are changing with it. OIStride Academy helps learners develop practical technology skills through structured, practitioner-led learning designed around application.

Fit Check

Is OIStride Academy right for you?

This is for you if:

  • You want structured, case-study-driven learning, not another self-paced video library you'll never finish.
  • You're switching careers, formalising real project management experience, or ready to ship something with AI.
  • You want direct access to a practitioner, not just a curriculum.

This may not be the right fit if:

  • You're looking for a passive credential with no live component.
  • You can't commit a few hours a week for the cohort duration.
  • You're looking for free training rather than a high-touch learning experience.

About the Founder

Learn from practitioners who do the work.

Obomhense Idemudia ("Jed") is a Senior Product Manager and Agile trainer whose work spans fintech, healthcare, and travel-tech, owning real product decisions and cross-functional delivery, not just training slides. He has trained and mentored professionals across Nigeria, Canada, the UK, the USA, and West Africa, and brings hands-on, AI-augmented ways of working into every session he runs.

Every OIStride Academy cohort is led by a facilitator trained and selected by our founder, delivering the same practical, case-study-driven standard he built the curriculum on.

The Academy's founder and facilitators bring experience across technology-enabled industries and professional environments.
