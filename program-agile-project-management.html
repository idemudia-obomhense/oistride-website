Agile Project Management

An 8-week, live cohort covering Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, and hybrid methodologies, with real project simulation from initiation to delivery.

Enroll Now, Choose Your Cohort Download Brochure
What You'll Walk Away With

Real capability, not just notes

  • → Run a live sprint in Jira: backlog, estimation, sprint boards, and burndown reporting.
  • → Build and defend a project schedule under real constraints: scope cuts, budget pressure, shifting stakeholders.
  • → Lead stakeholder and risk conversations through case studies drawn from real cross-industry scenarios.
  • → Walk into a PM or Scrum Master interview able to speak fluently about tools, frameworks, and trade-offs.
Curriculum

Eight weeks, module by module

1

Foundations of Project Management

What a PM actually does day to day: project life cycle, roles, and key terminology across the discipline. Case study: setting up a project right.

2

Planning, Communication, Cost & Risk

Scope, estimation, dependencies, and the critical path. Building a realistic schedule and budget, and managing stakeholder communication as constraints change. Case study: planning under real constraints.

3

Execution, Monitoring & Closure

Running kick-offs and managing a team through delivery. Tracking schedule, budget, and quality, then reporting progress and formally closing a project. Case study: execution to close-out.

4

Foundations of Agile & Scrum

The Agile Manifesto, its principles and values, and how Agile compares to Waterfall and hybrid approaches in practice. Case study: moving a team from Waterfall to Agile.

5

Advanced Scrum with Jira

Setting up projects and issue types in Jira, writing user stories, estimating with story points, and running sprint reports. Case study: running a live sprint in Jira.

6

Agile Project Management with Trello

Sprint planning and backlog refinement, burndown and burnup tracking, and Kanban boards for visual workflow management. Case study: visual workflow management.

7

Agile Metrics & Reporting

Velocity, burnup, and burndown as performance signals, and using metrics to drive continuous improvement and report effectively to stakeholders. Case study: continuous improvement in action.

8

Scrum Master Skills & Wrap-Up

Servant leadership and team facilitation, coaching Agile teams, removing blockers, and conflict resolution. Case study: servant leadership in action.

Who This Is For

This is for you if:

  • You're switching into project or product work from an adjacent role.
  • You already do PM work informally and want structured credentialing.
  • You want hands-on Jira and Trello fluency, not just theory.

This may not be the right fit if:

  • You want a fully self-paced, no-live-session experience.
  • You can't commit roughly 2 hours a week for 8 weeks.
